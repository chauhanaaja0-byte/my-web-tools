Free Offline Image Resizer

The Free Offline Image Resizer is a modern, high-performance web tool engineered to help photographers, designers, social media managers, and everyday users resize, crop, compress, and convert their images directly in their web browser. Built on client-side HTML5 Canvas and modern Web APIs, this tool executes all calculations and transformations locally on your computer, smartphone, or tablet. No images are ever uploaded to an external server or cloud service, guaranteeing 100% privacy, confidentiality, and blazing-fast processing speed.

How to Resize an Image

Resizing an image to specific dimensions or percentages is straightforward:

  1. Choose or Drop Your Photo: Drag and drop your image into the designated drop zone or click Choose Image.
  2. Select Dimensions: Enter your custom target width and height in pixels. Keep Lock Aspect Ratio enabled to prevent distortion, or choose from popular social media presets such as 1920 × 1080 (Full HD), 1080 × 1080 (Instagram Square), or 1200 × 630 (Facebook / Open Graph).
  3. Or Resize by Percentage: Switch to the percentage tab to scale your image down to 25%, 50%, 75%, or any custom proportion.
  4. Click Download: Save the resized photo instantly to your device.

How to Compress a Photo

Reducing file size without sacrificing noticeable visual quality is vital for faster website loading, email attachments, and online submissions:

  • Quality Slider: Adjust the compression slider between 10% and 100%. The default 85% provides an ideal balance of visual fidelity and substantial byte reduction.
  • Target File Size Optimization: Select a target size (such as 50 KB, 100 KB, 200 KB, or 500 KB). The tool will automatically run iterative compression algorithms to approximate your desired file size.

How to Crop an Image

Click on the ✂️ Crop Tool tab above the preview canvas. Select an aspect ratio (such as 1:1 Square, 16:9 Widescreen, 4:5 Portrait, or Free form). Drag the corner handles or move the crop box over the desired portion of your photo, then click Apply Crop to trim unwanted areas.

How to Convert JPG, PNG and WEBP

Switch between modern image formats seamlessly. Convert heavy PNG graphics to lightweight WEBP, transform JPG camera captures into PNG, or convert PNG logos into JPG. When converting transparent images into JPG format, you can select an elegant background fill color (White, Black, or a custom color).
