What is JPG?

JPG (or JPEG) is one of the most widely used image formats in the world, developed in 1992. While compatible with nearly all software, JPG relies on legacy compression algorithms that create noticeably larger files compared to modern next-gen web image formats.

What is WEBP?

WebP is a modern image format developed by Google specifically for the web. It provides superior lossless and lossy compression for images on the internet, creating images that are 25% to 35% smaller than comparable JPEGs at equivalent visual quality.

Why Convert JPG to WEBP?

Converting JPGs to WEBP dramatically speeds up website page load times, improves Google Core Web Vitals (LCP scores), reduces bandwidth consumption, and delivers faster loading experiences on mobile networks across the United States.
