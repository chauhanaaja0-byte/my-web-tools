Frequently Asked Questions

What is a text to logo maker? ▼

A text to logo maker is a specialized design tool that transforms typed brand names and optional taglines into polished, professional logos with custom typography, balanced layouts, color palettes, vector symbols, and downloadable export formats.

Is this logo maker completely free? ▼

Yes, this tool is 100% free to use. There are no subscriptions, no hidden watermark fees, and no paywalls. You can design, customize, and download unlimited logos in multiple formats.

Can I create a logo without an API? ▼

Yes! This entire logo generator executes locally inside your browser using standard HTML5, CSS3, Vector SVG, and Canvas APIs. No external server, cloud rendering pipeline, or AI API is called.

Can I download my logo as SVG? ▼

Yes. You can export a clean, scalable, standalone SVG file. The SVG is composed of native vector paths and scalable typography that you can edit in Figma, Adobe Illustrator, Inkscape, or embed directly into modern websites.

Can I make a transparent background logo? ▼

Yes. Simply toggle the Transparent Background option under Colors & BG. Both PNG and SVG exports will preserve full alpha channel transparency, allowing your logo to blend seamlessly over any background.

Privacy Policy: All logo generation and image processing take place entirely client-side in your browser. No brand names, taglines, or generated images are transmitted to or stored on any external server.
