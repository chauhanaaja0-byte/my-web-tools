In today's competitive United States job market, having an optimized, professional resume is your key to landing interviews. Our Free Resume / CV Maker is designed to help job seekers create pristine, recruiter-approved, and ATS-friendly resumes in minutes without any hidden fees or subscriptions.

How to Create a Resume Online (Step-by-Step)

  1. Enter Your Contact Information: Provide your full legal name, a professional email address, US phone number, city, and state. Include your customized LinkedIn URL or online portfolio.
  2. Write a Targeted Summary: Craft a 3–4 sentence executive summary highlighting your career achievements, core strengths, and quantifiable impact.
  3. Detail Your Work Experience: List your roles in reverse chronological order. Start each bullet point with powerful action verbs (e.g., Spearheaded, Optimized, Orchestrated) and include metrics ($ saved, % growth).
  4. Add Education & Certifications: Include your college degrees, graduation dates, and industry certifications (such as PMP, CPA, AWS, or SHRM).
  5. Highlight Core Skills: Incorporate both hard technical skills and soft interpersonal skills matching the job description keywords.
  6. Select a Template & Print to PDF: Choose from our 5 ATS-compliant templates and click "Print / Save as PDF" using standard US Letter dimensions (8.5 × 11 inches).

Resume Tips for US Job Seekers

  • Keep it ATS-Friendly: Avoid using multi-layered graphics, tables inside headers, or saving text as images. Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) scan for plain text and recognizable section titles.
  • Quantify Results: Instead of saying "Handled customer support", write "Resolved 60+ customer inquiries daily while maintaining a 98.4% CSAT rating".
  • Use Standard US Letter Format: US companies and recruiters expect 8.5 × 11 inch paper format with standard 0.5–0.75 inch margins.
  • Omit Personal Details: In the US, never include photos, marital status, birthdates, or full street addresses on a professional resume to comply with equal employment guidelines.

What to Include on a Resume

A standard American resume should feature distinct, easily scannable sections:

  • Header: Name, job title, phone, email, location (City, State), LinkedIn.
  • Professional Summary / Objective: High-impact career overview.
  • Professional Experience: Job title, employer name, dates, and bulleted accomplishments.
  • Education: Degrees, universities, honors, and graduation years.
  • Technical & Soft Skills: Tools, frameworks, and proficiencies.
  • Certifications & Licenses: Recognized professional credentials.
  • Projects & Awards: Notable standalone achievements and public repositories.
